## What it does

Deal Desk Assistant enforces pricing discipline without slowing reps down: instant guidance on discount limits, automatic approval routing for exceptions, and margin analytics per deal.

## Key features

- **Discount guardrails** — in-band deals auto-approve; exceptions route instantly.
- **Approval workflows** — Slack-based approvals with full deal context.
- **Margin analytics** — discount impact on margin visualized per deal and rep.
- **Win-rate by discount** — learn whether discounts actually win deals.
- **Non-standard terms radar** — flags legal/finance review triggers early.

## Who it's for

RevOps and sales leaders tired of margin leaking through ad-hoc discounting.
